MINECRAFT

The 10 Rules of Corita Kent

SURVIVAL

01. Find a place you trust

And then try trusting it for a while.

02. General duties of a student

Pull everything out of your teacher; pull everything out of your fellow students.

03. General duties of a teacher

Pull everything out of your students.

05. Be self-disciplined

This means finding someone wise or smart and choosing to follow them.

06. Nothing is a mistake

There's no win and no fail, there's only make.

07. The only rule is work

If you work it will lead to something.

EXPLORATION

04. Consider everything an experiment

08. Don't try to create and analyze at the same time

They are different processes.

09. Be happy whenever you can manage it

Enjoy yourself. It is lighter than you think.

10. We're breaking all the rules

Even our own rules. How do we do that? By leaving plenty of room for X quantities.
